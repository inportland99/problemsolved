How to play & dictionary
  • Write words of at least three letters. Connect neighboring tiles horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Do not reuse a tile within one word.
  • Q represents QU and counts as two letters. Plurals and ordinary word endings are included.
  • Two green timer tiles turn red about every 20 seconds. All 18 turn black at the end; the letters stay for discussion.
  • After the board is launched, your phone can lock. Wait for the physical letters to settle before playing. Internet and the backend scheduler must remain available.
  • Approximate timing only. A network failure can delay the ending sound. If status reports failure, agree an end to the round yourselves.
  • Scheduled quotes are skipped during the game. Other apps controlling the Vestaboard are outside this tool's control.
  • Suggestions use SCOWL size 35, American English (2020.12.07), excluding names, abbreviations, and source-listed taboo words. This is a smaller vocabulary, not an official Boggle dictionary or a guarantee of family-safe results.
